Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Sudoku 6 handles your information on iOS and Android. The current production bundle identifier/package is com.sudoku6.app.

1. Data stored on your device

Sudoku 6 stores gameplay-related information locally on your device to keep the app working properly.

  • Active game state such as puzzle progress, score, timer, and selected cell
  • User preferences such as sound, vibration, and gameplay guidance settings
  • Local statistics such as completed games, wins, best scores, and streaks
  • Locally stored leaderboard entries and weekly run records
  • Generated or cached sudoku puzzle data required by the app

2. Data we do not collect

The current build of Sudoku 6 does not require account creation and does not actively collect personal information from users.

  • No name, email address, or phone number collection inside the app
  • No microphone recording
  • No location, contacts, photo library, or file access
  • No in-app account profile or custom user registration

3. Permissions used

Sudoku 6 currently uses a small set of platform permissions and system capabilities for core gameplay support.

  • Internet and network access: used for platform compatibility, ad delivery, and consent flow support
  • Audio playback/system audio support: used for more reliable game sound playback
  • Vibration/haptics: used for tactile gameplay feedback where available
  • Advertising ID / AdServices: may be used by Google Mobile Ads SDK for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention where available

4. Leaderboards and advertising

  • Leaderboard entries are currently stored locally on the device
  • Leaderboard hooks exist in the codebase, but the current build does not submit scores to a live remote service
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK is active so the game screen can render a banner under the board and occasional end-of-win interstitial ads
  • Google UMP consent is requested before ad requests in regions where a consent form is required
  • Ad delivery and ad-related data handling are subject to AdMob and Google Mobile Ads SDK policies

5. Data retention

Local game data, statistics, cached puzzles, and preferences remain on the device until the app is removed or the app data is cleared by the user.

If you have privacy-related questions, you can contact the publisher through the support page below.
