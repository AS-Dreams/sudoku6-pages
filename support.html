Support

Need help with Sudoku 6: Paper Puzzle? This page is here for gameplay questions, bug reports, and general support.

Before Contacting Support

Please make sure you are using the latest version of the app from the App Store or Google Play. If the app behaves unexpectedly, try closing and reopening it first.

Contact Support

Email atillasefer@gmail.com for gameplay questions, bug reports, privacy requests, or help with either the iPhone or Android version. A GitHub account is not required to email us.

For a bug report, include your device model, app version, and the steps that led to the problem.

Report an Advertisement

To report an inappropriate or age-inappropriate ad, email the same address with the advertised product, the approximate time it appeared, and a screenshot if available. You can also use the ad's own information or reporting control when it is displayed.

Report an Issue

You can report issues through the public support tracker: Sudoku 6 Support Issues.

Privacy

Read the Privacy Policy for information about how the app handles data.
